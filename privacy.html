Privacy Policy

Simply AI Studio · Last updated October 8, 2026

Simply AI Studio ("the app") is the publishing tool Simply AI uses to post its own videos. Contact: simplyaishow@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • From TikTok, for the connected account only: its basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when the owner authorizes the app.
  • The status of each video the app posts (its publish ID and post status).

We do not collect data about viewers or any other TikTok users.

How we use it

Only to post Simply AI's approved videos to its own TikTok account and to confirm they were posted.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored encrypted in the operating system's keychain on Simply AI's own computer. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account stays connected. Revoking the app in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) ends access; we then delete the stored tokens. You can also ask for deletion at simplyaishow@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
