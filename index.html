AI news, explained simply. Simply AI makes short and long videos about new AI models and tools, and publishes them on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Simply AI Studio

Simply AI Studio is our in-house publishing tool. After the channel owner approves a finished video, the tool posts it to the channel's own accounts, including TikTok (@simplyaishow) through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is used only by Simply AI to post its own videos; it does not access other people's accounts.
